About Me

Electrical & Computer Engineering • Embedded Systems • Semiconductor Design

Bridging hardware, firmware, and engineering research

I am pursuing a Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Missouri–Kansas City (UMKC). My interests include embedded systems, firmware development, VLSI/ASIC design, RF and antenna engineering, signal processing, and connected devices.

Before graduate school, I worked at QualiZeal Technologies in firmware testing and software quality engineering. I validated embedded and IoT firmware across hardware configurations and built automated functional and regression tests using tools such as Selenium, Playwright, Java, and TestNG. This experience strengthened my approach to debugging, reliability, and verification.

My hands-on work spans STM32 microcontrollers, sensor interfaces and real-time control; CMOS/SRAM design and RTL-to-GDS workflows using Cadence tools; and RF/antenna simulation and characterization using CST Studio and MATLAB. I have also presented IEEE conference research on cross-domain cyber awareness using signal intelligence techniques.

My goal is to contribute to engineering teams developing dependable embedded products, advanced semiconductor systems, and next-generation communication technologies.
